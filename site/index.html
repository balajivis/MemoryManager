Built-in memory helps, but only inside the tool that has it. Switch apps and you're a stranger again.

How it works

  1. 01

    Bring it in.

    Import the chat history you already have, or save something as you go. Just say "remember this."

  2. 02

    Keep it in one place.

    MemoryManager organizes what matters, like your projects, preferences, decisions and the facts about you, into one memory you own. You can read and edit all of it.

  3. 03

    Use it everywhere.

    The AI tools you use pull in the right context when they need it, through MCP where the tool supports it. Every chat starts where you left off.

What changes when your AI remembers you

Better context.

Answers fit your project, your stack and your style, because the AI already knows them.

More accurate answers.

Less guessing at what you meant, and fewer confident answers to the wrong question.

Less re-explaining.

Skip the preamble. Shorter prompts mean fewer tokens spent setting the scene.

Your memory, your rules.

  • You own it.

    Your memory belongs to you, not to any AI vendor.
