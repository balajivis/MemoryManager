## What we collect

**Server logs.** Like most websites, our web server automatically records each request it receives. A log entry includes:
- your IP address, shortened before it's written: we drop the last part (the last octet of an IPv4 address, or everything after the first 48 bits of an IPv6 address), which makes it much harder to link a log entry to you
- the date and time
- the page or file requested, including any query string (for example, tracking parameters on a shared link)
- your browser's user-agent string
- the referring page, if your browser sends one
- the response status

We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure, for example to diagnose errors and stop abuse. Our server keeps two logs, an access log and an error log. Both store IP addresses with the last part removed, and both are deleted after 7 days. They never leave the server. {Publish only after devops and qa verify each of these sentences on the live server.}

**The waitlist.** If you join the waitlist, we store:
- your email address
- the date and time you signed up

We don't store your IP address or browser details with your sign-up.

## What we don't do

- We don't set cookies.
- We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or social media plugins.
- We don't load anything from third parties. Fonts are served from our own server.
- We don't sell or share personal information.
- We don't use your email address for anything other than the waitlist.
